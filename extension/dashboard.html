    What this page actually measures

    Each product is sampled on the buy side (Vinted and leboncoin: private sellers, where things are cheap) and on the resale side (eBay's official Browse API, Back Market, or a price you typed in). The acquisition price is the 15th percentile of the buy-side listings — what you could realistically pay, not the once-in-a-year cheapest — and the resale price is the median of the resale sample.

    Fees are subtracted (marketplace commission, payment processing, shipping you pay) before the margin is called a margin. That is why a 30% price gap is often only a 12% net margin.

    Resale references. A verdict is only as good as the number it is compared against, so every card states its reference: for each resale source, the median of the sample and how many listings that median came from. When no reference could be measured, the card says so and offers a search link instead of pretending — and a manual resale price in the catalogue always wins over the sample.

    The trend column is public-interest data: Wikimedia pageviews for the product's article (free, key-less, no scraping of social platforms), optionally GDELT news attention, plus the keywords you paste into Settings yourself. There is no API that returns "what is trending on TikTok", and this extension does not pretend otherwise — marketplace demand (favourites, listing volume, how fresh the listings are) is the signal with actual money behind it, and it carries a third of the score.

    Confidence is about sample size, not enthusiasm. A gorgeous margin computed from four listings is labelled not enough data, and stays that way.

    Price check

    One keyword, one marketplace, straight to a median — the same estimator the scans use, without creating a watch or a catalogue entry. Use it to sanity-check a reference price before you trust a verdict.

    What the numbers mean

    Median is the middle asking price after outliers are trimmed — the number to compare an offer against. IQR (Q1–Q3) is the middle half of the market: a listing below Q1 is genuinely cheap for that keyword, and one below the 15th percentile is what the buy side of an opportunity uses as its acquisition price.

    A median from fewer than eight listings is not a market, it is an anecdote, and the result says so rather than rounding it into confidence. Page-based marketplaces are read slowly, from your own session, so a check can take a few seconds.

    Catalogue

    The products one analysis run samples. Edit a keyword or a price band to match what you actually see, and add your own at the bottom. A manual resale price wins over the measured resale sample.

    Hand numbers over from ChatGPT, Claude — or a person

    A browser extension cannot be sent anything: it has no address an app can dial, and it is not allowed to listen. So an assistant hands its answer over one of two ways, and both land in the same place — Waiting for you, at the bottom of this page.

    1. Paste — press “Copy the instructions” below and paste them into the chat. The assistant answers with a block of JSON; paste its answer into the box. No account and no server, and it works with any assistant, including one running on your own machine.
    2. Live bridge — a small queue that was deployed for you. Paste its address into ChatGPT as a connector once; from then on the extension checks it every 15 minutes by itself.
    3. Then you decide — an import never changes a price, a payout or a verdict on its own. Each number waits with the page it came from, and Apply is a click you make.

    Option 1 — paste an answer

    Works today, with any assistant. The instructions tell it the two kinds of number this tool understands, so it cannot hand over an asking price labelled as a payout.

                Read this before you switch anything on
                
    
                  Two of these are documented APIs: eBay (free developer keyset, no seller account needed) and
                  anything you reach through a data provider you bring a key for. Everything else is a
                  page: the extension reads it, in a tab you already have open, at a slow pace, with no
                  user-agent spoofing, no proxy rotation and no CAPTCHA solving.
                
    
                
    
                  Sites whose search is client-rendered default to reads your tab: you run the search
                  yourself, the extension measures the page you are looking at. That is the mode that builds no URL and
                  makes no request of its own, and it is the one to prefer when a site is hostile to automation.
                
    
                
    
                  A terms note is not legal advice and it is not a promise. It is what each site's own developer material
                  and terms actually say, so you can make the call yourself. Facebook Marketplace is off by default because
                  Meta's terms explicitly forbid automated collection without permission.
